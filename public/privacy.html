Privacy Policy

Rise One Man Show — Field Management Software
Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Rise One Man Show, operated by Jacob Franks, a sole proprietor located in Marble Falls, Texas ("we," "us," or "our"), collects through the Service at riseonemanshow.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: admin@riseonemanshow.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When a company signs up, we collect the company name, and each user's name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form — we never store or see your plain-text password).

Business data you enter. The Service is a tool for managing your field-service work, so it stores the data you and your team put into it, including:

  • Your customers' names, addresses, and phone numbers
  • Job descriptions, schedules, and job reports
  • Photos uploaded to job reports
  • Hours worked and mileage records
  • Invoice details (line items, amounts, and totals)

Location data. If you start travel tracking for a trip, the Service collects GPS location from your device during that trip to calculate mileage. See Section 4 for details.

Billing information. Subscriptions are billed by our payment processor, Stripe. Your card number and payment details are entered into Stripe and handled by Stripe — they never touch or are stored on our servers. We receive and store limited billing records from Stripe, such as subscription status, plan, and the last four digits or brand of a card, so we can show your billing status in the app.

Basic technical information. Like most web services, our hosting systems automatically record standard log information (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps) needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect only to run the Service for you:

  • To create and manage your company account and user logins
  • To provide the core features: scheduling, job reports, invoicing, and mileage/hours tracking
  • To process subscriptions and show billing status
  • To provide customer support when you contact us
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the Service, and to fix problems

We do not use your business data or your customers' information for advertising, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

3. Service Providers (Processors)

We share information with a small number of service providers only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management, including the Stripe Billing Portal where you update your card or cancel. Stripe's handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.
  • Render — cloud hosting. The application and its database (which stores account information and the business data you enter) run on Render's infrastructure.

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the Service. We don't share your information with other third parties except as required by law (for example, a valid court order) or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the Service.

4. GPS and Location Data

  • Location is collected only during a travel leg you actively start in the app, and only for the purpose of calculating trip mileage.
  • Tracking is user-initiated: it runs when you (or a member of your team) start travel for a job, and it stops when the travel leg ends or the job begins.
  • We don't track location in the background outside of an active travel leg, and we don't sell or share location data.
  • Company Owners and Admins are responsible for informing their own team members that travel tracking records location during trips.

5. Cookies

The Service uses session cookies only — a cookie that keeps you signed in while you use the app. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. If you block cookies, you may not be able to stay signed in.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • We keep your account and business data while your account is active so the Service can function.
  • When a company account is cancelled or terminated, we delete that company's data — including customer information, job reports, photos, and user accounts — within 30 days, except where we're required to keep limited records (such as billing and transaction records held by Stripe, or records the law requires us to retain).
  • Backups may retain data for a short additional period before being overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
  • If you'd like your company's data deleted sooner, or have questions about deletion, contact us at admin@riseonemanshow.com.

7. Security

We use reasonable, industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls that wall each company's data off from other companies. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we can't guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password for your account.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction. You can view and update most account and business information directly in the app at any time.
  • Deletion. You may cancel your account at any time, which triggers the deletion process described in Section 6.
  • Your customers' information. If you are a customer of a business that uses Rise One Man Show and have questions about information that business holds about you, please contact that business directly — they control that data, and we'll support their handling of your request.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information; contact us and we'll honor applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is a business tool intended for users 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify account owners by email or in the app before the changes take effect, and update the effective date at the top of this page.
